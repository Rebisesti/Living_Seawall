The main points for our seawall that we want to cover with our calculations are deterioration and how much force our seawall can take before failing. We went with this direction because we want long term reliability and safety for marine life. Understanding how fast material deteriorates underwater will allow us to make assumptions on when repairs will be needed as well as understanding how much force our seawall can withstand to help support infrastructure.
